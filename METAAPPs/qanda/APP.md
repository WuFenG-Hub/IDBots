## When To Use

View the on-chain Q&A: the latest questions, the unanswered queue, and ZhiHu-style question pages with ranked answers. Clicking a `pin://` link to a question in chat opens the question page here.

## Pages

### Home (latest questions)
- `/qanda/app/index.html#/`

### Unanswered
- `/qanda/app/index.html#/unanswered`

### One question
- `/qanda/app/index.html#/q/<question-pinId>`
