MVA Motion Video Arena

Watch two code-rendered Motion Videos and pick the one you prefer. Choices queue locally and are submitted together as one public on-chain preference pin through the Browser identity you select. Author, model, and scores stay hidden until after you choose.

Queued comparisons

Submitting writes your choices and any reasons as one public pin on chain. Only deliberate choices are submitted — watch time and viewing behavior stay local and never enter the pin.

Motion Video Arena is a portable MetaApp. Signing identity is chosen in the Browser chrome; this app never signs by itself. Technical playback failures are never counted as negative votes.
